1. Purpose and Scope
AL SIRAJ DEVELOPERS is a real-estate town management and record-keeping system for towns, plots/shops, sales, installments, remaining payments, cash/banks, accounts, salaries, investors, contractors, agents, receipts, reports, approvals, local Excel records, cloud sync and CEO monitoring. It helps reduce mistakes and improve transparency, but final business supervision, verification and decisions remain the Client/Owner/CEO responsibility.
2. Two-Month Service Support
Mahad and Mahdi Developers will provide two months of software support from final handover/installation for bugs, errors, calculation issues caused by the software, and reasonable technical guidance. This does not include new major modules, major redesigns, hardware repair, internet issues, third-party outages, cloud provider problems, manual file changes, employee/accountant fraud, misuse, negligence, or new business requirements unless separately agreed and paid.
3. Data Storage, Backups and Hard Records
The system is local-first and may store data in local Excel files and sync with Supabase/cloud where configured. Local Excel files are an office-readable record/cache/backup. The Client must keep regular local backups, external/USB backups, printed important reports, and handwritten/manual hard records for critical property and financial transactions. The software must not be treated as the only business record.

Data issues may occur due to power failure, Windows corruption, hard disk/SSD failure, virus/malware, internet failure, wrong cleanup use, manual deletion/editing, cloud outage, third-party restrictions, multiple-PC conflict, user negligence or misuse.
5. Cloud Sync and Third-Party Services
Cloud sync is provided for backup, monitoring and CEO app connectivity, but depends on internet, Supabase, Firebase/FCM, credentials, storage and third-party service availability. Cloud-side data loss, outage, delayed sync, failed upload/download, account suspension, pricing/limit changes, API/policy changes, or cloud deletion by Client/staff is not the Service Provider responsibility. The Client must verify sync status regularly.
6. Offline Mode and Pending Approvals
Offline work may be supported so entries can continue without internet. Approval-required actions may remain pending until internet is connected and CEO review is completed. The Client/CEO/accountant must connect internet, review pending approvals, check pending sync, and prevent misuse of offline mode. The Service Provider is not responsible for loss caused by failure to connect internet, review pending items, or maintain backups.

Pending/rejected items should not affect totals; approved items may affect records according to business rules. The CEO/authorized person is responsible for reviewing approvals carefully. Receipts and reports are generated from entered data; if wrong or false data is entered, wrong receipts/reports may be produced.
